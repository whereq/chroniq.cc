# chroniq.cc is here — scheduling that respects your calendar

**Release 1.0 · August 2026**

Booking links are everywhere. What's missing is one that actually understands your
day. **chroniq.cc** is a scheduling tool built calendar-first: share a personal
link and let people book you automatically — inside a real calendar that knows
about holidays, lunar dates, the weather, and the meetings you've already got.

👉 **[chroniq.cc](https://chroniq.cc)**

---

## Why chroniq is different

Most schedulers treat your calendar as an empty grid to be filled. chroniq makes
the **calendar the home screen** — so the times you offer are grounded in the day
people actually live.

- 🗓️ **A real calendar workspace** — Month, Week, Day, and Year views, not just a
  booking form hidden behind a link.
- 🌦️ **Your day in context** — local holidays & global observances, the Chinese
  lunar calendar, weather, and daily context, each a layer you can toggle on or off.
- 🌍 **Speaks your language** — available in 8 languages: English, 中文, 日本語,
  한국어, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, and Français.

---

## What you can do today

- **Share one link.** `chroniq.cc/your-name` — anyone can open it and pick a time.
  No back-and-forth emails.
- **Never double-book.** Connect your Google calendar and chroniq only offers
  times you're genuinely free.
- **Set your rules.** Weekly hours, date overrides, buffers between meetings, and
  as many meeting types as you need.
- **Everyone stays in the loop.** Instant confirmations, plus easy reschedule and
  cancel links for your invitees.
- **Own your look.** A clean, fast, dark-or-light booking page — customizable, and
  on Pro, badge-free.
